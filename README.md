# Equity Research & Valuation

A collection of applied equity research and financial valuation projects covering listed companies across mining, technology and consumer-related sectors.

The repository demonstrates how company fundamentals, industry drivers and market expectations can be translated into valuation ranges and investment conclusions.

## Research Framework

- Business model and industry analysis
- Historical financial statement review
- Revenue, margin and cash-flow forecasting
- Discounted Cash Flow valuation
- WACC estimation and sensitivity analysis
- Comparable-company and trading-multiple analysis
- Investment thesis, catalysts and risk assessment
- Executive research reports and one-page summaries

## Tools & Concepts

- Excel financial modeling

## Repository Purpose

This repository is designed to demonstrate an end-to-end equity research process: moving from raw financial information to a documented valuation and decision-oriented investment thesis.

Third-party market information remains subject to its original provider's terms. Users should independently verify all assumptions and data before relying on the models.
